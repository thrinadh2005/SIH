## 📋 5. Step-by-Step YouTube Upload Instructions

1. **Log in to YouTube:**
   - Go to [studio.youtube.com](https://studio.youtube.com/) and sign in with your Google account.
2. **Click "Create" and then "Upload Videos":**
   - Click the **Create** button (top right) and select **Upload Videos**.
   - Drag and drop `TeamBuilders-SIH26138.mp4` from the `FINAL` folder.
3. **Fill in Details:**
   - **Title:** Paste the Option 1 title above.
   - **Description:** Copy and paste the complete description above (including the chapters with timestamps so YouTube automatically creates interactive video chapters).
   - **Thumbnail:** Click **Upload thumbnail** and choose `YouTube_Thumbnail_SIH26138.jpg`.
4. **Audience & Tags:**
   - Select **"No, it's not made for kids"**.
   - Click **Show More**, scroll down to **Tags**, and paste the comma-separated tags list above.
5. **Video Elements & Checks:**
   - Click **Next** through Video Elements and Checks (FFmpeg export is clean and copyright-free).
6. **Visibility & Publish:**
   - Under **Visibility**, select **Public** (or **Unlisted**).
   - Click **Save / Publish**.
7. **Copy Video URL:**
   - Copy your live YouTube video link and paste it into your Smart India Hackathon submission portal.
